Concrete Calculator

Estimate the exact volume of concrete you need for slabs, footings, columns and more. Works in metric and imperial units.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the length, width and depth of your concrete project.
  2. Choose your preferred units (metric or imperial).
  3. Click Calculate to see the exact volume you need.
  4. Add 5-10% waste allowance before ordering.

Formula Used

Volume = Length × Width × Depth

Example: 10 ft × 5 ft × 0.33 ft = 16.5 cubic feet = 0.61 cubic metres
