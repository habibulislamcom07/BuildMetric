Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Tools

01 Are all the calculators on BuildMetric completely free?

Yes. Every calculator in our directory is 100% free to use. There are no paywalls, no premium tiers, and no forced sign-ups required to see your results. The site is funded entirely through transparent display advertising.

02 Do I need to create an account to use the tools?

No account is required. You can use any calculator on the site immediately without providing an email address or creating a profile. Your calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser and are never stored on our servers.

03 How accurate are the calculations?

Our calculators apply universal mathematical formulas and are tested against reference cases in both imperial and metric units. Each tool is reviewed by a practising professional. However, results are estimates and should always be verified against your local building codes and professional judgement before construction begins.

04 Do the tools work on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes. BuildMetric is designed to be fully responsive. All calculators, input fields, and results are optimized for use on smartphones, tablets, and desktops. They are built to be used directly on-site.

05 Are the calculators updated for current building codes?

Yes. Where applicable, our tools reference current standards such as IRC 2021 and IBC for the US, BS 5534 and Building Regulations for the UK, and the NCC and AS 1684 for Australia. Every tool displays a version number and last updated date.

06 Can I use these tools for projects outside the US, UK, or Australia?

Yes, the mathematical calculations are universal. However, our code references and material guidance are specific to the US, UK, and Australia. If you are building elsewhere, you must apply your local jurisdiction's codes and standards to the results.

07 How do I request a calculator that isn't listed here?

You can email contact@buildmetric.org with the subject "Calculator Suggestion". Describe the specific calculation or scenario you need help with. We prioritize new tools based on real project requests from contractors and builders.

Still have a question?

Email contact@buildmetric.org — every message is read by a real person, and we reply within two business days.

Verified Formulas · Code-Aware

Every calculator in this directory is built from formulas that can be derived from first principles. We do not guess. We test every tool against reference cases in both imperial and metric units before it is published.

More importantly, our tools are reviewed by quantity surveyors, structural engineers, and licensed contractors. We know that a calculation error on site costs money and compromises safety, so we treat accuracy as a structural requirement, not a feature.
