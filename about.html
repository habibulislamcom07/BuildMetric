About BuildMetric

BuildMetric is a free online platform offering 200+ accurate, easy-to-use calculators for concrete, paint, roofing, flooring, tiles, lumber and more. We are built for builders, contractors, engineers and DIY enthusiasts across the world — from weekend home improvers to professional tradespeople working on site every day.

Who We Are

BuildMetric is a small, independent team of builders, engineers and software developers who share a common frustration: construction maths is often unnecessarily complicated, and the tools available online are frequently inaccurate, out of date, or buried under advertising and pop-ups.

We come from different corners of the construction world. Some of us have spent years on job sites pouring concrete, framing walls and laying tiles. Others have worked in engineering offices calculating loads, materials and costs. And some of us are simply DIY enthusiasts who got tired of guessing how many bags of concrete to buy.

Together, we decided to build something better — a free, fast, accurate calculator platform that respects the user's time and intelligence. BuildMetric is the result.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to give every builder, contractor and DIYer free access to accurate, transparent construction calculators — anywhere in the world, in any unit system, without sign-ups or paywalls.

We believe that accurate calculation should never be locked behind a subscription. Whether you are a professional contractor estimating a multi-storey project or a homeowner pouring your first patio slab, you deserve the same precision, the same clear explanations and the same respect.

That is why every calculator on BuildMetric:

  • Is completely free — no sign-ups, no paywalls, no hidden charges.
  • Shows the formula — so you can verify the result yourself.
  • Works in both imperial and metric — so it is useful anywhere in the world.
  • Includes a worked example — so you can check your understanding.
  • Explains the standard specifications — so you know what "4-inch slab" or "16-inch stud spacing" actually means.

Why BuildMetric Exists

Construction projects succeed or fail on numbers. Order too little concrete and you end up with a cold joint that weakens the entire slab. Order too much and you have wasted money sitting in a wheelbarrow. Cut lumber to the wrong length and an entire day's work is lost. BuildMetric exists to eliminate that guesswork — permanently.

Every calculator on this site uses the same formulas trusted by professional contractors, civil engineers and tradespeople across the world. We do not approximate. We do not round aggressively. We give you the exact figure, then explain how it was calculated so you can verify it yourself before you spend a single pound, dollar, rupee or euro.

Unlike generic calculator websites that bury the maths behind a black box, BuildMetric shows every step. Each tool page includes the underlying formula, a worked example, standard specifications, and answers to the questions people actually ask. You do not just get a number — you understand it.

We also believe that construction knowledge should be global. Whether you are pouring a driveway in Texas, laying tiles in Manchester, building a deck in Sydney, or renovating a kitchen in Toronto, our calculators work in both imperial and metric units and are designed for an international audience.

Our Promise: We do not sell your data. We do not accept payment to recommend products. We do not hide the formula behind a paywall. BuildMetric is — and will always remain — free, transparent and independent.

Our Methodology

Every calculator on BuildMetric is built according to four strict principles. These are not marketing slogans — they are the rules we apply to every formula, every label and every result we publish.

1. Verified Formulas

Each calculation is based on standard industry formulas used by contractors, civil engineers and building authorities across the world. We do not invent shortcuts. We do not approximate without saying so. Where there is a widely accepted standard — such as 4 inches for a patio slab, or 10% waste allowance for pre-mixed concrete — we follow it and cite it clearly on the page.

2. Original Explanations

Every page includes unique, human-written content explaining the concept, the formula, the worked example and common use cases. We do not copy from other websites. We do not use auto-generated text. We do not spin content. If a calculator exists on BuildMetric, someone on our team sat down and wrote the explanation from scratch — so that a first-time DIYer can understand it and a professional can verify it.

3. Global Units

Calculators support both imperial units (feet, inches, pounds, gallons) and metric units (metres, centimetres, kilograms, litres). You can switch between the two with a single click, and your preference is remembered for the next time you visit. This makes every tool usable whether you are building in New York, London, Sydney, Toronto, Dubai, Cape Town or Mumbai.

4. Mobile-First Design

Every calculator is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktops. Forms are keyboard-accessible, labels are properly associated with inputs, and results are announced to screen readers. We believe useful tools should be available to everyone, regardless of device or ability.

Our 7-Phase Growth Roadmap

BuildMetric is being built in seven carefully planned phases. Each phase adds new tools, deeper explanations and broader coverage — so quality is never sacrificed for speed. Our long-term commitment is to reach over 200 calculators, covering every major construction and DIY calculation need worldwide.

Phase 1 — Foundation Tools (24 Calculators)

The most searched calculators go live first. Concrete, paint, drywall, roofing, flooring, tiles, lumber, insulation and similar essentials form the backbone of the platform. These tools cover the highest-volume searches in the construction niche and provide the strongest foundation for organic growth.

Phase 2 — Traffic-Building Calculators (40 Calculators)

Room area, wall area, roof pitch, gutter sizing, shingles, mortar, grout, garden beds and soil calculators are added. These target long-tail keywords that bring in steady, consistent organic traffic from homeowners and small contractors.

Phase 3 — Professional & Trade Tools (56 Calculators)

Framing, joists, rafters, beams, plumbing, electrical, HVAC and detailed cost estimators are introduced. This phase establishes BuildMetric as a serious daily resource for professional contractors and trade specialists.

Phase 4 — Unit Converters & Engineering Calculators (60 Calculators)

Feet to metres, cubic yards to cubic feet, board-feet conversions, rebar spacing, concrete mix ratios, load calculations and slope gradients. These serve both DIYers working on weekend projects and engineers working across imperial and metric systems.

Phase 5 — Energy & Efficiency Calculators (40 Calculators)

Solar panel sizing, battery backup, generator sizing, LED savings, insulation payback, heat loss and energy cost calculators for the rapidly growing green-building audience and homeowners seeking long-term savings.

Phase 6 — Smart Home & Safety Calculators (20 Calculators)

Security camera coverage, Wi-Fi planning, fire sprinkler demand, smoke detector placement and fall-protection calculators for modern homes and code-compliance work.

Phase 7 — International & Metric Expansion (20 Calculators)

Metric-first versions of every major calculator, plus regional cost estimators, so builders in Europe, Asia, Africa, the Middle East and Latin America get the same accuracy and detail as those using imperial units.

Total target: 200+ calculators, each with original explanatory content, verified formulas, worked examples and a clear FAQ section.
