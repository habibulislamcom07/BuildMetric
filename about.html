About BuildMetric

BuildMetric is a free construction calculator platform with 300+ tools across 12 categories. It exists because the arithmetic of construction should not be hidden behind a signup wall, an affiliate link, or a blurry PDF from 2009.

Built for Accuracy, Trust and Global Use

Every BuildMetric calculator follows the same three principles. These principles are not marketing slogans — they are the rules we apply to every formula, every label and every result we publish.

1. Verified Formulas

Each calculation is based on standard industry formulas used by contractors, civil engineers and building authorities across the world. We do not invent shortcuts. We do not approximate without saying so. Where there is a widely accepted standard — such as 4 inches for a patio slab, or 5% waste for pre-mixed concrete — we follow it and cite it clearly on the page.

2. Original Explanations

Every page includes unique, human-written content explaining the concept, the formula, the worked example and common use cases. We do not copy from other websites. We do not use auto-generated text. We do not spin content. If a calculator exists on BuildMetric, someone sat down and wrote the explanation from scratch so that a first-time DIYer can understand it and a professional can verify it.

3. Global Units

Calculators support both imperial units (feet, inches, pounds, gallons) and metric units (metres, centimetres, kilograms, litres). You can switch between the two with a single click, and your choice is remembered for the next time you visit. This makes every tool usable whether you are building in New York, London, Sydney, Toronto, Dubai, Cape Town or Mumbai.

Our Independence Pledge

BuildMetric is independent. We do not sell building materials, tools or contractor services. We do not accept payment to recommend any product or brand. Our only goal is to give builders, contractors and DIYers free, reliable calculators they can trust — with transparent formulas and no commercial pressure.

Accessibility Commitment

Every calculator is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers. Forms are keyboard-accessible, labels are properly associated with inputs, and results are announced to screen readers. We believe useful tools should be available to everyone, regardless of device or ability.

Accuracy Disclaimer

While we take great care to ensure every formula is correct, construction conditions vary from site to site. Soil type, moisture, temperature, material quality and local building codes can all affect the amount of material you actually need. Always add a 5–10% waste allowance and consult a qualified professional before placing a final order or beginning structural work.

Our 7-Phase Growth Roadmap

BuildMetric is being built in seven carefully planned phases. Each phase adds new tools, deeper explanations and broader coverage — so quality is never sacrificed for speed. Our long-term commitment is to reach over 300 calculators, covering every major construction and DIY calculation needed worldwide.

Phase 1 — Foundation Tools (24 Calculators)

The most searched calculators go live first. Concrete, paint, drywall, roofing, flooring, tiles, lumber, insulation and similar essentials form the backbone of the platform. These tools cover the highest-volume searches in the construction niche and provide the strongest foundation for organic growth.

Phase 2 — Traffic-Building Calculators (40 Calculators)

Room area, wall area, roof pitch, gutter sizing, shingles, mortar, grout, garden beds and soil calculators are added. These target long-tail keywords that bring in steady, consistent organic traffic from homeowners and small contractors.

Phase 3 — Professional & Trade Tools (56 Calculators)

Framing, joists, rafters, beams, plumbing, electrical, HVAC and detailed cost estimators are introduced. This phase establishes BuildMetric as a serious daily resource for professional contractors and trade specialists.

Phase 4 — Unit Converters & Engineering Calculators (60 Calculators)

Feet to metres, cubic yards to cubic feet, board-feet conversions, rebar spacing, concrete mix ratios, load calculations and slope gradients. These serve both DIYers working on weekend projects and engineers working across imperial and metric systems.

Phase 5 — Energy & Efficiency Calculators (40 Calculators)

Solar panel sizing, battery backup, generator sizing, LED savings, insulation payback, heat loss and energy cost calculators for the rapidly growing green-building audience and homeowners seeking long-term savings.

Phase 6 — Smart Home & Safety Calculators (20 Calculators)

Security camera coverage, Wi-Fi planning, fire sprinkler demand, smoke detector placement and fall-protection calculators for modern homes and code-compliance work.

Phase 7 — International & Metric Expansion (20 Calculators)

Metric-first versions of every major calculator, plus regional cost estimators, so builders in Europe, Asia, Africa, the Middle East and Latin America get the same accuracy and detail as those using imperial units.

Total target: 300+ calculators, each with original explanatory content, verified formulas, worked examples and a clear FAQ section.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is BuildMetric? +
BuildMetric is a free construction calculator platform with 300+ tools across 12 categories — concrete, paint, drywall, roofing, flooring, tiles, lumber, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, solar and cost estimation. Every calculator runs entirely in your browser, requires no signup, and shows the formula it uses so you always know how the number was calculated.
2. Are BuildMetric calculators really free? +
Yes. Every calculator on BuildMetric is 100% free with no signup, no trial period, and no email gate. We are supported by clean, non-intrusive advertising and optional B2B supplier connections that you never have to use. There are no paywalls and no hidden fees.
3. Do BuildMetric calculators work in both metric and imperial units? +
Yes. Every calculator supports both imperial units (feet, inches, pounds, gallons) and metric units (metres, centimetres, kilograms, litres). You can switch between the two with a single click on any calculator page, and your preference is saved for future visits. This makes every tool usable whether you are building in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, Europe, Asia or Africa.
4. How accurate are BuildMetric calculators? +
Calculators use published formulas from recognised standards bodies and material suppliers. Waste factors can be toggled (typically 5–15%) to account for cutting, spillage and breakage. Results are usually within 3–5% of a manual take-off on straightforward geometry. For structural work, always have final quantities verified by a licensed engineer.
5. How many calculators does BuildMetric offer? +
BuildMetric currently offers over 300 calculators across 12 categories: Concrete & Masonry, Interior & Finishing, Exterior & Structure, Landscaping & Outdoor, Lumber & Framing, Electrical & HVAC, Plumbing & Piping, Roofing & Siding, Painting & Wallpaper, Road & Driveway, Cost Estimators and Solar & Energy. New calculators are added regularly.
6. Can I use BuildMetric calculators on mobile devices? +
Yes. Every calculator is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers. The interface is mobile-first, with touch-friendly controls sized for gloved hands on a job site. Pages load in under 1.5 seconds on 4G and continue to work offline after the first load.
7. Do I need to create an account to use the calculators? +
No. BuildMetric does not require any account, login or email address. All calculators work instantly when you open the page. We do not store your calculations or personal information on our servers. Your unit preference (Imperial or Metric) is saved locally in your own browser, so it remembers your choice next time — but this data never leaves your device.
