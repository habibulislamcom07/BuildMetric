Agreement in Plain Language

Terms of service are usually the most-ignored document on the internet. People scroll to the bottom, click "Accept", and move on — because most terms are written to protect the company that publishes them, not the person who reads them. We think that is a mistake. We also want to be clear about what these terms are not. They are not a warranty that everything on the site is error-free — no honest website can offer that. And they are not a contract for engineering services — BuildMetric is a publisher, not a consultancy. If anything in this document is unclear — or if you have a question that the terms do not directly answer — please email contact@buildmetric.org.
