Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information BuildMetric collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It also explains how advertising and analytics partners — including Google AdSense — operate on this website. By using buildmetric.org, you agree to the practices described on this page.

1. Introduction

BuildMetric ("we", "our", "us") operates the website buildmetric.org and provides free online construction and DIY calculators. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information with transparency, care and respect.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we may collect when you visit our website, how we use that information, and the rights you have over it. It applies to all visitors, regardless of where you are located — whether you are in the United States, United Kingdom, European Union, Australia, Canada, India or anywhere else in the world.

By accessing or using buildmetric.org, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect two broad types of information: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically as you browse.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

BuildMetric does not require you to create an account, log in, or share personal information to use any of our calculators. However, you may voluntarily provide information in the following situations:

  • Contact form: If you use our Contact page, you may provide your name, email address, subject and message. This information is sent directly from your own email client to contact.buildmetric@gmail.com — it is not stored on our servers.
  • Email communication: If you email us directly, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message. We retain this correspondence only as long as necessary to respond and resolve your inquiry.
  • Social media: If you interact with us on YouTube or Facebook, those platforms collect data according to their own privacy policies. We only see the information those platforms choose to share with us.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit buildmetric.org, certain information is collected automatically by our servers, our analytics tools and our advertising partners. This may include:

  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, device type (desktop, tablet, mobile), screen resolution and language preference.
  • Log data: IP address (which may be anonymised), pages visited, time spent on each page, referring URL and timestamps.
  • Location data: Approximate geographic location derived from IP address (typically at country or region level). We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Usage data: Which calculators you use, how often you use them, and which buttons you click. This helps us understand which tools are most valuable.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: Small text files placed on your device to remember preferences, measure traffic and serve relevant advertising. See Section 4 for details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following legitimate purposes:

3.1 To Provide and Improve Our Service

We use usage data to understand which calculators are most helpful, identify bugs and improve the accuracy of our tools. This helps us prioritise new features and ensure the site remains fast and reliable for everyone.

3.2 To Respond to Your Inquiries

If you contact us by email or through the contact form, we use the information you provide solely to respond to your question, resolve your issue or act on your feedback. We do not add you to any mailing list or send unsolicited emails.

3.3 To Display Advertising

BuildMetric is supported by advertising, primarily through Google AdSense. Advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. This helps keep our calculators completely free for all users. See Section 5 for full details on advertising.

3.4 To Measure Traffic and Performance

We use analytics tools — including Google Analytics — to understand how visitors find and use the site. This information is aggregated and anonymised, and is used only to improve content, performance and user experience.

3.5 To Comply with Legal Obligations

In rare circumstances, we may be required to disclose information in response to a lawful request by public authorities, a court order or applicable law. We will only do so when legally required, and we will never sell your personal information to third parties.

Our Promise: We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not build user profiles. We do not send marketing emails. If you contact us, we use your information only to reply.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

BuildMetric uses cookies and similar technologies (such as web beacons, pixels and local storage) to improve your experience and to support advertising. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit.

4.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic site functionality, such as remembering your preferred unit system (imperial or metric) so you do not have to select it every time you visit.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and choices to personalise your experience on future visits.
  • Analytics cookies: Used by Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the site — which pages are popular, how long people stay, and how they found us.
  • Advertising cookies: Used by Google AdSense and its partners to serve ads that may be relevant to your interests.

4.2 Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View the cookies stored on your device
  • Delete individual cookies or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites or from all websites
  • Set your browser to notify you whenever a cookie is set

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and other websites. For instructions on managing cookies, visit your browser's help section (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge or Opera).

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

BuildMetric is a free website supported by advertising. We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our pages. This section explains how Google AdSense operates and what it means for your privacy.

5.1 Third-Party Vendors

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

5.2 The DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on buildmetric.org. Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. The DART cookie does not track personal information such as your name, email address, physical address or telephone number.

5.3 Opting Out of Personalised Advertising

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at google.com/settings/ads. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu (for users in the European Union).

If you opt out of personalised advertising, you will still see ads on BuildMetric — but the ads will be less relevant to your interests.

5.4 Advertising Partners

In addition to Google AdSense, other third-party advertising networks may serve ads on our site. Each of these partners has its own privacy policy and its own methods of collecting data. We do not have control over the cookies or tracking technologies used by these third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any advertising partners you encounter.

Important: BuildMetric does not have access to, nor control over, the cookies used by Google AdSense or other advertising partners. Ad personalisation is managed entirely by Google and its partners according to their own policies.

6. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use BuildMetric. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit the site, what pages they visit and what other sites they used before coming to ours.

We use the information from Google Analytics only to improve our website and the calculators we offer. Google Analytics collects the IP address assigned to you on the date you visit the site, rather than your name or other identifying information. We do not combine the information collected through Google Analytics with personally identifiable information.

Google's ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics about your visits to this site is restricted by the Google Analytics Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can prevent Google Analytics from recognising you on return visits by disabling cookies in your browser.

7. Third-Party Links

BuildMetric may contain links to external websites, including social media platforms (YouTube, Facebook), educational resources and reference material. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. When you click a link and leave our website, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website you visit.

8. Data Retention

BuildMetric does not operate a database of user accounts, and we do not store personal information on our servers. The only personal information we receive is what you voluntarily send us by email or through the contact form on our Contact page.

Email correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve your inquiry, and in some cases for a short period afterwards for record-keeping and follow-up purposes. We do not retain contact form submissions because they are sent directly from your email client to ours — they are never stored on our web servers.

Aggregated analytics data (such as page views, session duration and referring sources) is retained by Google Analytics according to Google's default retention settings. We do not have access to raw user-level analytics data beyond what Google provides in aggregate reports.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have specific legal rights regarding your personal information. BuildMetric respects these rights and will make every reasonable effort to help you exercise them.

9.1 Rights Under GDPR (European Union & UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access: You can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can request that we correct any inaccurate data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): You can request that we delete any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to restrict processing: You can request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability: You can request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: You can object to our processing of your data for specific purposes.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You can file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because BuildMetric does not operate a user account system or store personal data on its servers, most of these rights apply only to email correspondence you have sent us. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 13.

9.2 Rights Under CCPA (California)

If you are a resident of California, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know: You can request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to delete: You can request deletion of personal information we have collected from you.
  • Right to opt out of sale: You can request that we do not sell your personal information. BuildMetric does not sell personal information, so this right is automatically respected.
  • Right to non-discrimination: You will not be discriminated against for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

9.3 Other Jurisdictions

Users in other jurisdictions (including Australia, Canada, India, Brazil, Japan and others) may have additional rights under local data protection laws. We are committed to respecting those rights as well. If you have questions about your rights, please contact us.

10. Children's Privacy

BuildMetric is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our calculators are designed for adult professionals, contractors and DIY enthusiasts.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such information from our records.

11. International Data Transfers

BuildMetric serves users worldwide. When you use our website, some of the third-party services we rely on — including Google Analytics and Google AdSense — may transfer data to servers located outside your country of residence, including the United States. These services operate under their own privacy frameworks and safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Standard Contractual Clauses and other mechanisms recognised by applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements or industry standards. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information. Your continued use of BuildMetric after any changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
