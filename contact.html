How Can We Help?

BuildMetric is built for builders, contractors, engineers and DIY enthusiasts who want accurate numbers without guesswork. Whether you are pouring your first concrete slab in your backyard or managing multi-site construction work, our calculators are designed to give you reliable results — and our team is here to support you at every step.

If you have a question that is not answered anywhere on the site, please do not hesitate to contact us. We read every message personally and respond to every legitimate inquiry. There is no automated bot replying — a real person reads your message and replies.

Questions About Our Calculators

Our library currently includes over 200 free calculators covering nine major categories: Concrete & Masonry, Interior & Finishing, Exterior & Structure, Landscaping & Outdoor, Lumber & Framing, Energy & Efficiency, Plumbing & Electrical, Unit Converters, and Roofing & Waterproofing. Each tool is designed to be self-explanatory, but if a calculation seems unclear, you need help interpreting a result, or you want to know why we use a specific formula, please send us a message.

We are always happy to explain the maths behind our calculators. If you want a worked example for a specific scenario — say, a footing for a particular soil type, or tile spacing for an unusual layout — just ask and we will provide one. We believe understanding the numbers is just as important as getting them.

Report an Issue

Even with careful testing, mistakes can happen. If you notice an incorrect result, a formatting problem, a broken link or a calculator that behaves unexpectedly, please report it. To help us fix the issue quickly, include:

  • The name of the calculator (for example, "Concrete Calculator")
  • The exact values you entered
  • The result you received
  • The result you expected, and how you calculated it
  • A screenshot, if possible

Every reported issue is investigated personally. If a formula is wrong, we correct it within days and note the change publicly. Accuracy is our highest priority — without it, the tools are useless.

Suggest a New Calculator

Our roadmap already includes hundreds of planned calculators, but we always welcome new ideas from real users. If there is a calculation you perform often in your trade or DIY work and wish existed as a free tool, let us know. We prioritise suggestions that come with a clear use case: what you are calculating, why it matters, and how often you need it.

Many of the tools currently on the site were added because users asked for them. Your suggestions genuinely shape what we build next.

Partnership & Advertising Inquiries

BuildMetric reaches a global audience of builders, contractors, engineers and DIYers. If you represent a tool brand, a materials supplier, a construction software company or an educational platform and you are interested in collaborating, we are open to relevant partnerships.

Please note: we do not accept paid placements that compromise our editorial integrity. We do not recommend products we have not personally evaluated. Any advertising on the site is served through Google AdSense and clearly labelled. If you have a partnership idea that fits our values, reach out with details and we will respond honestly.

Feedback & Suggestions

Beyond bugs and new tool requests, we welcome broader feedback. Is there a category you would like to see expanded? A page that could be clearer? A feature that would make your work easier? Every message helps us make BuildMetric better for everyone who uses it.

We do not use surveys, pop-ups or intrusive feedback widgets. The best way to tell us what you think is simply to email us. We read every message and take every suggestion seriously.

Our Response Time

We aim to respond to all messages within 24 to 48 hours. During peak periods (such as the start of construction season in spring), responses may take slightly longer, but we never ignore a legitimate message. If your matter is urgent, please include "Urgent" in the subject line and we will prioritise it.

Our Commitment to You

Independent & Free

BuildMetric is independent. We do not sell building materials, tools or contractor services. We do not accept payment to recommend any product or brand. Every calculator on this site is completely free to use, with no sign-ups, no paywalls and no hidden charges.

When you contact us, we only receive the information you choose to send. We do not store your name, email address or message on our servers — the form uses your own email client to send the message directly to us. For full details, please read our Privacy Policy.

Real People, Real Replies
