Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we get most. If yours is not here, send us a message above.

1. How quickly will I hear back? +
We read every message and reply within 2–3 working days. For calculator corrections, please include the tool URL and the standard you are working to — it speeds up the review enormously. Messages that arrive on weekends are answered on the next working day.
2. How do I report a wrong formula? +
Email us the tool URL, the input values you used, the result you expected, and the standard you are working to (ACI, BS EN, AS, CSA, IS or Eurocode). We will verify against the source clause and issue a fix if required. Every accepted correction is published in the tool's changelog.
3. Can I request a new calculator? +
Yes. Email us with the calculator name, what it should compute, and any standard or formula you already know about. Requests that come from working tradespeople are prioritised. New tools typically ship within 4–8 weeks from acceptance. If your request is declined, we will tell you why.
4. Do you accept guest posts or sponsored content? +
No. BuildMetric does not publish guest posts, sponsored reviews, or paid placements inside calculator content. Advertising appears only in clearly labelled, separate slots and never inside results. If a supplier wants to reach our audience, the only route is the B2B form on tool and category pages — clearly labelled, opt-in, and never inside a calculation.
5. How do I become a supplier partner? +
Supplier enquiries are handled through the B2B form on any tool or category page, or by emailing contact.buildmetric@gmail.com with "Supplier enquiry" in the subject line. Include your material category, region, and the order sizes you handle. We respond to every supplier enquiry within 2 working days.
