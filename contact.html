Frequently Asked Support Questions

You might find the answer you are looking for instantly in our help center below.

How do I report a calculation error?
If you believe a calculator is giving an incorrect result, please use the contact form in Phase 2 below. Select "Report a Bug" and provide the following details: The name of the calculator, the exact numbers you inputted, and the result you got vs the result you expected. Our engineering team will review it within 24 hours.
Are your calculators accurate for commercial projects?
Yes, our formulas are based on standard engineering guidelines (IRC in the US, Building Regs in the UK). However, we always recommend that for large commercial projects, you cross-verify our results with a licensed structural engineer or your material supplier before placing massive orders.
Can I request a custom calculator for my business?
Absolutely. We build custom estimation tools and API integrations for construction companies, material suppliers, and hardware brands. If you want a calculator embedded on your own website, select "Business Inquiry" in the form and we will send you our pricing structure.
