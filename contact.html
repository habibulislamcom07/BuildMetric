Introduction

How We Handle Enquiries

BuildMetric receives a few hundred messages a month — and every one of them is read by a member of our editorial or engineering team. That is a deliberate choice. We are not a faceless content farm, and we do not route enquiries into a chatbot that answers with generic boilerplate. If you take the time to write to us, we take the time to read what you wrote.

Most messages fall into one of four categories: corrections (you spotted an error in a calculator or guide), technical questions (you need clarification on a formula, unit conversion or code reference), advertising and partnership enquiries, and general feedback (you have a suggestion, a complaint or a compliment). Each is handled by a different person on the team, and each has a slightly different response rhythm — but all messages receive a reply within two business days, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays in the US, UK and Australia.

To help us respond quickly and accurately, please include the following in your message where applicable: the URL of the page you are writing about; a screenshot or clear description of what you observed; what you expected to see instead; and, for corrections, any source references (code clauses, standard sections, manufacturer data, or published research). The more specific your message, the faster we can verify and act. A one-line message like "the calculator is wrong" takes far longer to resolve than "on the roof pitch calculator, entering rise 6 and run 12 returns 26.57 degrees, but I believe it should be 26.5651" — because the second message tells us exactly where to look and what comparison to make.

For calculator corrections, we aim to investigate within 48 hours, respond within two business days, and where warranted, publish a fix with a version note on the affected page. We log every reported issue in an internal tracker, and significant corrections are disclosed in the page's update history. For advertising enquiries, we will send you our current media kit, audience data and available placements — but note that we do not accept sponsored content that requires us to alter calculator formulas, editorial conclusions or rankings, and we do not run ads for products we believe are unsafe or misrepresented.

One important limitation: we cannot provide project-specific advice. If you write to us asking whether a particular pitch, beam size, foundation depth or material is appropriate for your project, we will not be able to answer — that decision must be made by a licensed professional who has seen the site and reviewed the drawings. We are happy to explain the underlying formula, point you to the relevant code section, or clarify how our calculator works, but we cannot substitute for the judgement of an engineer of record.

Finally, a note on privacy. Email correspondence with BuildMetric is treated as confidential within our team. We do not sell, rent or share your email address or message contents with third parties, and we do not add correspondents to marketing lists unless they explicitly opt in. Messages may be retained internally for editorial and audit purposes (for example, to log a correction and its resolution), and full details of how we handle personal data are set out in our Privacy Policy. If you would prefer your message not be retained beyond the response, just say so in your email and we will accommodate it.

The Process

What Happens After You Write

No black box. Here is exactly what happens to your message from the moment it arrives to the moment it's resolved.

  1. 1

    Your message lands in our inbox

    Within minutes

    Every email to contact@buildmetric.org arrives in a shared inbox read by our editorial team. We triage each message by category — correction, technical, advertising, or general — and assign it to the person most qualified to respond.

    • Automated acknowledgment sent (check spam if missing).
    • Honeypot filter removes suspected spam automatically.
  3. 2

    A real team member reviews it

    Within 48 hours

    Corrections go to the engineering reviewer who owns that calculator. Technical questions go to the editor who wrote the surrounding guide. Advertising enquiries go to our partnerships lead. Where a fix is needed, we test the reported case against reference values before touching the published tool.

    • Internal issue logged with timestamp and reporter details.
    • Cross-checked against IRC / BS / AS editions as applicable.
  5. 3

    You get a reply and a resolution

    Within 2 business days

    You receive a direct written response, including — where a fix is warranted — what changed, the version number, and the date of the update. Validated corrections appear in the page's version history, and where relevant we credit the reporter (with permission).

    • Fix deployed with a visible version note on affected pages.
    • No newsletter sign-up — your email stays with us only.

Writing Tips

Common Enquiry Topics & Message Templates

The messages we can act on fastest follow a simple pattern: what you saw, what you expected, and where you saw it. Below are templates you can copy and adapt for the most common cases.

Template 01

Calculator Correction

Subject: Correction — [calculator name]

Page: [full URL]

Input: rise = X, run = Y

Saw: [result returned]

Expected: [result you expected]

Source: [code/standard/screenshot]

Concise, verifiable, and easy to reproduce. This format gets a fix faster than any other.

Template 02

Technical Question

Subject: Question — [topic]

Page: [URL of related guide]

Question: [specific question]

Context: [what you've already checked]

Code: [edition / clause if relevant]

The more context you give, the more specific our answer can be. Avoid open-ended "how do I…" questions without any detail.

Template 03

Advertising Enquiry

Subject: Advertising — [brand]

Brand: [company + URL]

Product: [what you sell]

Placements: [display / affiliate / other]

Budget: [rough range]

Timeline: [start date]

Please note: we don't accept sponsored content that requires us to alter formulas, conclusions or rankings.

Do This

  • Include the exact URL of the page or calculator you're writing about.
  • Describe inputs and expected output — a reproducible example saves a full round-trip.
  • Attach a screenshot if the issue is visual or ambiguous.
  • Cite your source — code clause, standard section, or manufacturer data.
  • Say what you expected — a comparison makes the fix obvious.

Avoid This

  • "The calculator is wrong" with no further detail — impossible for us to investigate.
  • Project-specific "is this safe?" questions — we can't certify individual projects.
  • Sending confidential drawings or client info — email is not a secure channel.
  • Attaching very large files — link to cloud storage instead.
  • Sending the same message multiple times — one clear email is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Contacting Us

Seven direct answers about response times, what we can and cannot help with, advertising, privacy and contributing to BuildMetric.

01 How quickly will BuildMetric respond to my message?

We aim to reply to every message within two business days (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays). Typical response times are around 24 hours for US and UK enquiries, 24–48 hours for Australian enquiries, and 48–72 hours for other regions. Corrections to calculators are prioritised and usually reviewed within 48 hours. If you have not received a reply within three business days, please check your spam folder before resending.

02 Can BuildMetric give me advice about my specific construction project?

No. BuildMetric is a publisher of general construction information, not a consultancy. We cannot provide project-specific structural, safety, compliance or quantity surveying advice, and we cannot certify drawings, calculations or material takeoffs for permit applications. For any decision that affects the safety, compliance or buildability of your project, please engage a licensed engineer, architect, building surveyor or quantity surveyor in your jurisdiction who has reviewed the actual site and drawings.

03 What is the best way to report an error in a calculator?

Email contact@buildmetric.org with the subject "Correction — [calculator name]". Include the full page URL, the exact inputs you entered, the output you saw, the output you expected, and any source reference such as a code clause, standard section or manufacturer data. A reproducible example lets us verify and fix the issue much faster than a general report. Where a fix is warranted, we publish it with a visible version note and credit the reporter (with permission).

04 Do you have a phone number I can call?

No. BuildMetric operates entirely by email to keep responses documented, searchable and consistent across the US, UK and Australian teams. Email correspondence also protects the privacy of both parties and lets us attach screenshots, code references and version notes accurately. For urgent matters, mark your subject line with the word URGENT and we will triage it first.

05 How do I get in touch about advertising on BuildMetric?

Email ads@buildmetric.org with the subject "Advertising — [brand]". Please include your company and URL, the product or service you wish to promote, the placement type you're interested in (display, sponsored content, affiliate), a rough budget range and your intended start date. We will send our current media kit, audience data and available placements within two business days. Note: we do not accept sponsored content that requires us to alter calculator formulas, editorial conclusions or rankings.

06 Will my message or email address be shared with anyone?

No. Email correspondence with BuildMetric is treated as confidential within our editorial and engineering team. We do not sell, rent or share your email address or the contents of your message with third parties, and we do not add you to any marketing list unless you explicitly opt in. Messages may be retained internally for editorial and audit purposes — for example, to log a correction and its resolution — in line with our Privacy Policy. If you would prefer your message not be retained beyond the response, simply request this in your email.

07 Can I submit a guest article or contribute a guide to BuildMetric?

We occasionally accept guest contributions from practising professionals — engineers, surveyors, architects, licensed contractors — who can demonstrate verifiable credentials and relevant field experience. We do not accept paid placements, sponsored "guest posts" or SEO link-insertion offers, and we do not publish content written by AI without a named human reviewer. If you believe you have a contribution that meets our standards, email contact@buildmetric.org with your credentials, a short writing sample and a proposed topic. We respond to accepted pitches only.
