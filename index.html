Common Questions About Our Tools

Everything you need to know about how our calculators and guides work.

Are all the calculators on BuildMetric completely free?
Yes, 100%. Every calculator and guide on BuildMetric is free to use. There are no paywalls, no premium tiers, and no forced sign-ups required to view results.
Do I need to create an account to use the tools?
No account is required. You can use any calculator immediately without entering an email address, creating a password, or verifying a phone number.
How accurate are the calculations?
Our calculators apply universal mathematical formulas and are tested against reference cases. The logic is also reviewed by licensed contractors and structural engineers before publication to ensure real-world accuracy.
Do you support both Metric and Imperial units?
Yes. You can toggle between Imperial (feet/inches) and Metric (meters/centimeters) on almost every tool. The math adjusts automatically based on your region.
Do your guides reference local building codes?
Yes. Where applicable, our technical guides reference the IRC (International Residential Code) for the US, BS (British Standards) for the UK, and AS (Australian Standards) for Australia.
Can I suggest a topic for a new calculator?
Absolutely. If you are stuck on a specific formula, material calculation, or code requirement, email us at contact@buildmetric.org. We prioritise new tools based on what our readers actually need.
Is my calculator data saved or tracked?
No. All calculator inputs and results stay 100% in your browser. We do not transmit, store, or track the measurements you enter.

Still have a question? Email contact@buildmetric.org — every message is read by a real person.
