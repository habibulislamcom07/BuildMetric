Why BuildMetric Exists

Numbers decide whether a build succeeds or quietly fails. Order too little concrete and you get a cold joint that weakens the entire slab — a defect that shows up two years later as a hairline crack you can't explain. Cut lumber two inches short and an entire day's framing is wasted. Over-order by fifteen percent "just to be safe" and your margin disappears into a lumber yard's profit line.

Professional estimators solve this with spreadsheets, code books, and twenty years of field experience. BuildMetric exists to put the same accuracy in every builder's hands — whether you're a licensed general contractor pricing a 40-unit development in Denver, a landscaper quoting a garden bed in Auckland, or a first-time homeowner tiling a single bathroom in Dublin.

Every calculator on this site follows the same discipline. Verified formulas used by civil engineers and building authorities worldwide. Transparent math that shows the full working, not just a result — so you can check it before you spend a single dollar, pound, or euro. And regional notes that flag where US, UK, Canadian, Australian, and New Zealand standards diverge, because a slab spec in Phoenix is not the same as one in Perth.

We do not sell building materials. We do not accept payment to recommend a product. We do not hide the formula behind a black box. If a number looks wrong to you, we want you to be able to trace it back, step by step, and tell us why. That is the standard every tool on this site is held to — and it is why contractors, site foremen, and serious DIY builders keep coming back.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What Is BuildMetric?

BuildMetric is a free online platform offering 200+ construction and DIY calculators for concrete, paint, roofing, flooring, tiles, lumber and more. Every tool explains the formula it uses so you can verify the result before ordering materials. The site is built for builders, contractors, engineers and DIYers worldwide.

2. Are BuildMetric calculators really free?

Yes. Every calculator on BuildMetric is completely free and will remain free. There is no paywall, no premium tier and no signup required to use any tool. The site is supported by unobtrusive display advertising, which keeps the calculators open to everyone without compromising the accuracy or transparency of the results.

3. Do BuildMetric calculators work in both metric and imperial units?

Yes. Every calculator supports both imperial units (feet, inches, pounds, gallons, cubic yards) and metric units (metres, centimetres, kilograms, litres, cubic metres). You can switch between the two with a single click, and your preference is remembered for your next visit — so you don't have to set it again on every page.

4. How accurate are BuildMetric calculators?

Each calculator uses standard industry formulas that are accepted by contractors, civil engineers and building authorities in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. We do not round aggressively and we do not hide the maths. That said, real-world site conditions — soil type, moisture, waste, material variability — can shift the actual quantity you need. Always add a 5–10% waste allowance and confirm with a qualified professional before ordering or beginning structural work.

5. How many calculators does BuildMetric offer?

BuildMetric currently offers more than 200 calculators, and the library is actively growing toward 300. Tools are organised into clear categories — concrete and masonry, interior and finishing, exterior and structure, landscaping and outdoor, lumber and framing, energy and efficiency, plumbing and electrical, unit converters, and roofing and waterproofing — so you can find the right tool in seconds.

6. Can I use BuildMetric calculators on mobile devices?

Yes. Every calculator is designed mobile-first and works on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops. The interface is touch-friendly, keyboard-accessible and screen-reader compatible, so you can run estimates right on the job site without needing to switch to a desktop.

7. Do I need to create an account to use the calculators?

No. Every calculator on BuildMetric works instantly without any account, login or email. If you want to save your calculations, export them as PDF or CSV, or share them with a teammate, you can optionally create a free account — but it is never required to use the site.
