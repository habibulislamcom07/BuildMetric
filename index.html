Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using our construction calculators.

Are BuildMetric calculators 100% accurate for commercial projects?
Our calculators use standard industry formulas (IRC for US, Building Regs for UK) and include a recommended waste factor. While they are highly accurate for estimations, we always advise professional contractors to verify final quantities with their suppliers before purchasing large commercial orders.
Do I need to create an account or pay to use these tools?
Absolutely not. BuildMetric is 100% free. You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or enter a credit card. We believe professional tools should be accessible to everyone. We keep the site running through non-intrusive advertisements.
Can I use these calculators on my mobile phone on the job site?
Yes! BuildMetric is built as a mobile-first application. It loads lightning-fast and works perfectly on smartphones and tablets, even on slower mobile networks at construction sites.
How do you handle US (Imperial) vs UK (Metric) measurements?
Every tool on our platform includes a unit toggle. If you are in the US, you can input feet and inches. If you are in the UK or Australia, you can switch to meters and centimeters with one click. The output automatically adjusts to local standards.
Do your tools account for material waste and spillage?
Yes. For most materials like concrete, paint, and tiles, our calculators automatically add a standard waste factor (usually 5% to 10%) to your total. This ensures you order enough material to cover uneven surfaces, cuts, and spillage.
How often are the calculators updated?
We continuously review our formulas to ensure they align with the latest building codes and material standards. When new standards are introduced in the US, UK, or Australia, we update our backend logic accordingly.
Do you provide cost estimates or just material quantities?
We offer both. While most calculators focus on material quantities (like cubic yards or bags), our "Cost Estimators" category provides localized price ranges for labor and materials in the US ($) and UK (£). These are perfect for creating quick preliminary budgets.
