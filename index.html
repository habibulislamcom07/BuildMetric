Why BuildMetric

Construction Tools That Respect Your Time

Most construction calculators on the internet were not built by construction people. They were built by marketers, SEO agencies or content farms — sites that collect a search term, bolt on a formula, wrap it in aggressive advertising and hope you click the right button before you give up in frustration. Some of them are wrong. Most of them do not explain why the answer is what it is. Almost none of them tell you the assumption they made to get there.

BuildMetric is different, and the difference is the team behind it. Every calculator on this site is written by or reviewed by someone who has held a tape measure on a real site — quantity surveyors, structural engineers, licensed roofing contractors, and technical editors who have spent their careers translating engineering into plain language. We do not publish a tool unless we can explain the formula behind it, defend the units it uses, and point to the code or standard that governs the answer. If we cannot do that, the tool does not ship.

Our commitment to accuracy is structural, not promotional. That means calculators are built from formulas that can be derived from first principles — not from guesswork or "industry averages" pulled off a random forum post. It means results are tested against reference cases in both imperial and metric before publication. It means every tool is reviewed by a practising professional in the trade it serves. And it means when someone reports an error — which happens, and is expected — we investigate it, fix it publicly, and log the change in the page's version history with a date and a note. You can see what we have changed and when.

Our commitment to transparency is not a marketing line either. The site is funded by advertising and affiliate commissions, both of which are clearly disclosed. There is no paywall, no forced sign-up, no gated "premium" result. We do not sell your data. We do not track your calculator inputs — the maths runs entirely in your browser, and the numbers you type never reach our servers. And we do not accept paid reviews, sponsored rankings or sponsored editorials. Advertisers can buy display placements. They cannot buy our opinion, our formula, or a favourable position in our results.

We are honest about scope. BuildMetric publishes for three markets — the United States, the United Kingdom and Australia — because those are the three regulatory environments our team knows intimately. Where codes, units and standards differ between those markets, we say so. Where we cannot provide local guidance outside those markets, we say that too. The underlying mathematics of a roof pitch or a concrete pour is universal, but the code context, approval pathway and material guidance are not. We would rather be useful in three markets than approximate in ten.

And we are honest about limits. A calculator is a mathematical instrument, not a design authority. Our results are a starting point for professional judgement, not a substitute for it. Every page on this site says so, because we have seen what happens when a calculator result is treated as an engineering opinion. If your project involves load-bearing elements, fire separation, waterproofing or anything with life-safety consequences, the answer is not "check the calculator" — it is "hire a licensed professional who has seen the site." Our tools help you ask better questions of that professional. They do not replace them.

If that sounds like the kind of tool you have been looking for, welcome. If it sounds like the kind of tool you have been waiting to build yourself — get in touch. We are always interested in hearing from practising professionals who want to help us raise the standard.

How It Works

From Formula to Published Tool

Every calculator on BuildMetric passes through the same four-stage process. Nothing bypasses it — not even small updates to an existing tool.

  1. 01

    Formula

    Derivable

    The tool starts with a formula that can be derived from first principles — usually simple geometry or algebra. If we cannot explain it in one sentence and prove it with a worked example, it does not proceed to the next stage.

  2. 02

    Verification

    Tested

    The tool is tested against reference cases in both imperial and metric. Rounding is checked against construction precision standards — typically two decimal places for angles, whole numbers for quantities. If outputs do not match, we do not ship.

  3. 03

    Professional Review

    Human

    A practising professional — quantity surveyor, structural engineer or licensed contractor — reviews the tool, the formula and the surrounding guide for accuracy against the current code edition. Nothing publishes without this sign-off.

  4. 04

    Publication

    Dated

    The tool is published with a visible version number and date. Every subsequent change — formula correction, unit fix, code reference update — is logged in the page's version history, publicly, so you can always see what changed and when.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About BuildMetric

Seven direct answers about pricing, privacy, review standards, accuracy, regional coverage and how to report an issue or suggest a new calculator.

01 Are BuildMetric calculators really free, and will they stay free?

Yes. Every calculator and written guide on BuildMetric is free to use, with no paywall, no forced sign-up and no gated results. The site is funded through clearly marked display advertising and, on some product references, affiliate commissions. There are no "premium" tiers, no trials, and no intention to introduce one. Advertising partners and affiliates have no influence over calculator formulas, editorial conclusions or rankings. If that funding model ever changes — for example, if we introduce a paid feature — it will be alongside the free tools, never instead of them.

02 Do you store the numbers I enter into your calculators?

No. Every calculator on BuildMetric runs entirely in your browser. The values you enter — dimensions, quantities, weights — are never transmitted to our servers, never logged, and never associated with you in any way. The maths happens locally on your device. Even if you copy a result to your clipboard, that action stays on your device unless you choose to email it to us or post it elsewhere yourself. There is no cookie, script or analytics event that records what you typed into a calculator.

03 Who reviews the calculators, and what are their credentials?

Every calculator is reviewed before publication by at least one practising professional in the trade it serves — quantity surveyors, structural engineers, or licensed roofing and framing contractors, depending on the tool. The reviewing group has combined field experience across the United States, United Kingdom and Australia. Reviews are not just a formality: they check the formula, the worked examples, the unit conversions and the code references against the current published edition. When a new tool is published, the review process is logged with a date and a named reviewer role. If you want to know who specifically reviewed a given page, email contact@buildmetric.org.

04 Are BuildMetric calculators accurate enough to rely on for a construction quote?

The mathematical output of a BuildMetric calculator is accurate because it is derived from published formulas and tested against reference cases in both imperial and metric. However, a construction quote also depends on site conditions, waste allowances, labour rates, material availability and local code requirements, none of which a calculator can fully account for. Use our outputs as a starting point, then adjust for waste and confirm with a professional takeoff before committing to a contract price. For anything involving structural, fire, waterproofing or life-safety consequences, a licensed professional must review the outcome before construction begins.

05 Why do you only cover the USA, UK and Australia?

Because our team has genuine field experience in those three markets — surveying in the UK, framing in the US and reviewing Australian code on project sites. The underlying mathematics is universal, so users anywhere can use our calculators. What we cannot honestly provide outside those three markets is regional code context, local approval guidance or jurisdiction-specific material recommendations. Rather than claim "global coverage" and deliver a lower standard, we have chosen to be explicit about the three markets we know and cover them properly.

06 Can I use BuildMetric if I live outside the USA, UK or Australia?

Yes. The site is accessible globally and the underlying mathematics is universal. However, our content is specifically written and reviewed for the United States, United Kingdom and Australian markets — including code references, unit defaults and material guidance. If you are outside those three markets, you are welcome to use the calculators, but you must apply your own jurisdiction's codes, standards and local approval pathways. We cannot provide regional code context, local approval guidance or jurisdiction-specific material recommendations for other markets.

07 How do I report an error or suggest a new calculator?

Email contact@buildmetric.org. For a correction, use the subject "Correction — [calculator name]" and include the page URL, the exact inputs you entered, the output you saw, the output you expected, and any source reference (code clause, standard section or manufacturer data). A reproducible example lets us verify and fix the issue much faster than a general report. For a new calculator suggestion, use the subject "Calculator Suggestion" and describe the scenario you would want to solve — a real project example is the fastest way to get it prioritised. We acknowledge all reports within 5 business days.

Still have a question?

Email contact@buildmetric.org — every message is read by a real person, and we reply within two business days.
