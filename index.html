    Why BuildMetric Exists

    Construction math shouldn't require a $3,000 software licence.

    In 2026, a licensed concrete contractor in Texas still opens a spreadsheet from 2011 to price a slab. A renovation crew in Manchester re-does the same paint takeoff by hand — three times, because the client changed the colour. A homeowner in Sydney gambles on how many solar panels will actually break even before 2035. BuildMetric was built to end that.

    We are a small team of structural engineers, licensed general contractors and full-stack developers who got tired of watching good tradespeople lose margin to bad math, and watching homeowners get quoted numbers nobody could justify. Every calculator on this site was written from the primary code document — not scraped, not summarised, not "inspired by." When a tool tells you a 4-inch slab needs 1.63 cubic yards of concrete, that number is traceable to ACI 318-19 mix-volume equations and a documented 10% waste factor — not a black box.

    Built for real jobsites, not demos

    Field crews work in the rain, on ladders, with wet gloves and one bar of signal. BuildMetric calculators load in under two seconds on 3G, use 44-pixel minimum touch targets, and cache aggressively so the numbers you just entered survive a lost connection. Unit toggles between Imperial and Metric sit one tap away, because a Canadian contractor quoting a US client needs both in the same bid package.

    Regional codes, regionally correct

    A footing sized to ACI 318-19 is not the same as one sized to BS EN 1997 or AS 3600. Rebar lap lengths differ. Concrete cover differs. Wind and seismic load factors differ. Our geo-detection engine reads your browser locale first, confirms with a secure IP lookup, and stores your choice for 30 days so returning visitors land directly on the correct code set — USD, GBP, AUD, CAD, INR and seven more currencies all handled natively.

    Free, and staying free

    BuildMetric has no subscription tier, no "pro" paywall, no forced email capture, and no affiliate links inflating our recommendations. The site is funded by non-intrusive display advertising and a supplier directory where manufacturers pay for visibility — never for influence over the numbers you see. If we ever change that model, we'll say so on this page before we change it anywhere else.

    What you get on every tool page

    • The calculator itself — instant, no click-to-reveal, no interstitials.
    • The formula — written out, with the governing standard cited.
    • A fully worked example — so you can verify the math by hand.
    • Seven real FAQs — the questions contractors actually ask, in 80–120 words each.
    • Related tools — because a slab estimate usually needs rebar and formwork next.

    If BuildMetric saves you one bad pour, one over-ordered pallet, or one angry client call, it has done its job. Tell us what to build next — contact.buildmetric@gmail.com goes straight to the engineers, not a support queue.
