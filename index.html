BuildMetric provides 200+ accurate, easy-to-use calculators for concrete, paint, drywall, roofing, flooring, tiles, lumber and more. Every tool shows the exact formula it uses, so you always know how the numbers are calculated — no guesswork, no wasted materials, no surprises on the job site.

Why BuildMetric Exists

Construction projects succeed or fail on numbers. Order too little concrete and you end up with a cold joint that weakens the entire slab. Order too much and you have wasted money sitting in a wheelbarrow. Cut lumber to the wrong length and an entire day's work is lost. BuildMetric exists to eliminate that guesswork — permanently.

Every calculator on this site uses the same formulas trusted by professional contractors, civil engineers and tradespeople across the world. We do not approximate. We do not round aggressively. We give you the exact figure, then explain how it was calculated so you can verify it yourself before you spend a single pound, dollar, rupee or euro.

Unlike generic calculator websites that bury the maths behind a black box, BuildMetric shows every step. Each tool page includes the underlying formula, a worked example, standard specifications (like slab thickness or tile spacing), and answers to the questions people actually ask. You do not just get a number — you understand it.
