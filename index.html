Why BuildMetric Exists

Construction projects succeed or fail on numbers. Order too little concrete and you end up with a cold joint that weakens the entire slab. Order too much and you have wasted money sitting in a wheelbarrow. Cut lumber to the wrong length and an entire day's work is lost. BuildMetric exists to eliminate that guesswork — permanently.

Every calculator on this site uses the same formulas trusted by professional contractors, civil engineers and tradespeople across the world. We do not approximate. We do not round aggressively. We give you the exact figure, then explain how it was calculated so you can verify it yourself before you spend a single pound, dollar, rupee or euro.

Unlike generic calculator websites that bury the maths behind a black box, BuildMetric shows every step. Each tool page includes the underlying formula, a worked example, standard specifications, and answers to the questions people actually ask. You do not just get a number — you understand it.

Whether you are pouring a driveway in Texas, laying tiles in Manchester, building a deck in Sydney, or renovating a kitchen in Toronto, our calculators work in both imperial and metric units and are designed for a global audience.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is BuildMetric? +
BuildMetric is a free construction calculator platform with 300+ tools across 12 categories — concrete, lumber, roofing, plumbing, electrical, solar, cost estimation and more. Every calculator runs entirely in your browser, requires no signup, and shows the formula it uses. Whether you are a professional contractor, a site engineer, or a homeowner planning a weekend project, you can enter your dimensions, get an instant answer, and understand exactly how the number was calculated.
2. Are BuildMetric calculators really free? +
Yes — every calculator on BuildMetric is 100% free with no signup, no trial period, no credit card, and no email gate. We are supported by clean, non-intrusive advertising and optional B2B supplier connections that you never have to use. There are no paywalls, no upsells inside results, and no hidden fees. If a future feature ever changes that, it will be clearly labelled and separate from the core tools.
3. Do BuildMetric calculators work in both metric and imperial units? +
Yes. Every calculator supports both imperial units (feet, inches, pounds, gallons) and metric units (metres, centimetres, kilograms, litres). You can switch between the two with a single click on any calculator page, and your preference is saved for future visits. This makes every tool usable whether you are building in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, Europe, Asia or Africa. Regional standards such as ACI, BS EN, AS, CSA, IS and Eurocode are also built in and detected automatically.
4. How accurate are BuildMetric calculators? +
Calculators use published formulas from recognised standards bodies and material suppliers. Waste factors can be toggled (typically 5–15%) to account for cutting, spillage and breakage. Results are usually within 3–5% of a manual take-off on straightforward geometry. For structural work — beams, columns, footings, anchors — always have final quantities and member sizes verified by a licensed engineer. The calculators are accurate; they are not a substitute for a stamped drawing.
5. How many calculators does BuildMetric offer? +
BuildMetric currently offers over 300 calculators across 12 categories: Concrete & Masonry, Interior & Finishing, Exterior & Structure, Landscaping & Outdoor, Lumber & Framing, Electrical & HVAC, Plumbing & Piping, Roofing & Siding, Painting & Wallpaper, Road & Driveway, Cost Estimators and Solar & Energy. New calculators are added regularly based on user requests and trade demand.
6. Can I use BuildMetric calculators on mobile devices? +
Yes. Every calculator is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers. The interface is mobile-first, with touch-friendly controls sized for gloved hands on a job site. Pages load in under 1.5 seconds on 4G and continue to work offline after the first load — because basements, tunnels and rural sites do not have reliable connectivity. Every input control is at least 44 pixels tall, and every number is set in a monospaced font so digits line up and misread zeros are less likely.
7. Do I need to create an account to use the calculators? +
No account is needed. Saved results are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. You can export any result as a PDF for offline use, or copy a permalink that reopens the same tool with your inputs pre-filled. Nothing is uploaded to our servers, and clearing your browser data clears your saved results — which is the correct privacy trade-off for job-site numbers.
