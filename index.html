How It Works

From Formula to Published Tool

Every calculator on BuildMetric passes through the same four-stage process. Nothing bypasses it — not even small updates to an existing tool.

  1. 01

    Formula

    Derivable

    The tool starts with a formula that can be derived from first principles — usually simple geometry or algebra. If we cannot explain it in one sentence and prove it with a worked example, it does not proceed.

  2. 02

    Verification

    Tested

    The tool is tested against reference cases in both imperial and metric. Rounding is checked against construction precision standards. If outputs do not match, we do not ship.

  3. 03

    Professional Review

    Human

    A practising professional — quantity surveyor, structural engineer or licensed contractor — reviews the tool and the formula for accuracy against the current code edition.

  4. 04

    Publication

    Dated

    The tool is published with a visible version number and date. Every subsequent change — formula correction or code update — is logged in the page's version history, publicly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About BuildMetric

01 Are BuildMetric calculators really free, and will they stay free?

Yes. Every calculator and written guide on BuildMetric is free to use, with no paywall, no forced sign-up and no gated results. The site is funded through clearly marked display advertising and, on some product references, affiliate commissions. There are no "premium" tiers, no trials, and no intention to introduce one.

02 Do you store the numbers I enter into your calculators?

No. Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. The values you enter are never transmitted to our servers, never logged, and never associated with you. There is no cookie or script that records what you typed.

03 Who reviews the calculators, and what are their credentials?

Every tool is reviewed by a practising professional (quantity surveyors, structural engineers, or licensed contractors). Reviews check the formula, unit conversions, and code references against the current published edition.

04 Are BuildMetric calculators accurate enough to rely on for a construction quote?

The math is highly accurate, but a construction quote depends on site conditions, waste allowances, and local codes. Use our outputs as a starting point, then adjust for waste and confirm with a professional takeoff.

05 Why do you only cover the USA, UK and Australia?

Because our team has genuine field experience in those three markets. Rather than claim fake "global coverage", we focus our code references and guidance where we can guarantee accuracy.

06 Can I use BuildMetric if I live outside the USA, UK or Australia?

Yes. The math is universal. However, you must apply your own jurisdiction's codes, standards and local approval pathways, as we cannot provide regional code context for other markets.

07 How do I report an error or suggest a new calculator?

Email contact@buildmetric.org. For corrections, use subject "Correction" and include the page URL, inputs, and expected output. For new tools, use "Calculator Suggestion".
