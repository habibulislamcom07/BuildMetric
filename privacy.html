Introduction

Our Privacy Commitment

Most privacy policies are written to protect the company that publishes them, not the person who reads them. They are dense, full of hedge words, and structured to say as little as legally possible while sounding thorough. We think that is backwards — especially on a site used by tradespeople and professionals who do not have time to decode legal jargon between site visits. This policy is written in plain language on purpose, and it is written to be understood rather than merely survived.

The short version is simple: we collect very little, we keep it for as short a time as possible, and we do not sell it. BuildMetric does not require you to create an account, does not ask for payment information, and does not have a login system with stored profiles. You can use every calculator on this site without telling us who you are. If you email us, we receive what you chose to send. If you browse, we receive aggregated technical data about the visit — the same kind of data that virtually every website on the internet collects, and nothing more invasive than that.

The calculators themselves are designed to be stateless. When you enter dimensions, weights or quantities into a roof pitch calculator or a concrete volume estimator, the maths happens in your browser. Your inputs are not transmitted to our servers, and they are not stored, shared or associated with you in any way. The only exception is if you explicitly choose to submit a message through our contact form or send us an email — in which case, the contents of that message become part of our support correspondence and are handled as described later in this document.

We operate in three jurisdictions — the United States, the United Kingdom and Australia — and we have designed this policy to meet or exceed the requirements of the laws that apply to each. That includes the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988. Where the laws differ, we apply the strictest standard that is compatible with local requirements, so that a user in any of our three markets receives at least the minimum protection they are entitled to — and often more.

We also want to be honest about what this policy cannot do. It cannot retroactively change the fact that advertising networks and analytics providers process some data on our behalf. It cannot guarantee that no breach will ever occur, because no honest policy can. It cannot eliminate the fundamental reality that the internet is built on third-party services — CDNs, font hosts, ad exchanges — some of which you will interact with indirectly every time you load a page. What it can do is tell you exactly which services those are, what they process, and what controls you have. That is what the remaining sections of this document do.

If you have read this far and still have questions — or if you have a request about your data that is not covered — please email contact@buildmetric.org. We read every message, respond within two business days, and treat privacy requests with the same seriousness as reported calculator errors. Privacy is not a tick-box exercise for us; it is part of the same commitment to accuracy and honesty that defines everything else on this site.

Section 01

Information We Collect

We keep data collection to the minimum required to operate the site safely, securely and reliably. We do not have an account system, we do not ask for your name or address to use a calculator, and we do not use fingerprinting or invasive tracking scripts. What we do collect falls into three categories: information you give us directly, information collected automatically by our servers, and information collected by third-party services we use.

1.1 Information you give us directly

You only provide personal information when you voluntarily contact us. That means:

Method What We Receive Why
Contact form Name, email, subject, reason, message, page URL (optional) To respond to your enquiry
Direct email Your email address, message body, attachments To respond and log corrections
Calculator feedback Whatever you type in the feedback field (if offered) To improve the tool

We do not collect your calculator inputs. Values you enter into any calculator on this site are processed entirely within your browser and never transmitted to our servers. If a calculator displays a "Copy result" or "Share result" feature, it only copies text to your clipboard — nothing leaves your device unless you choose to send it to us or post it yourself.

1.2 Information collected automatically

Like virtually all websites, our servers and CDN record basic technical data about every page request. This typically includes:

  • IP address — logged by our hosting provider, retained briefly for security and abuse prevention.
  • User agent — browser type and version, operating system.
  • Referrer URL — the page or search engine that led you to us.
  • Requested URL and timestamp — what was loaded and when.
  • HTTP status and response time — for performance monitoring.

This data is aggregated and used for security monitoring, debugging and capacity planning. It is not linked to any individual user profile.

1.3 Information we do not collect

To be equally clear about what is off-limits, BuildMetric does not knowingly collect: payment information (we have no paid products), government identification, biometric data, precise geolocation (only coarse country-level from CDN logs), health information, or any special category data under GDPR Article 9. We do not have a login system, so we do not store passwords. We do not run session-replay tools or heatmap software that records keystrokes.

Key Point

Calculator inputs never leave your browser. Your use of the tools is between you and your device.

Section 03

Cookies & Tracking

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They are used for many different purposes — remembering preferences, maintaining security, measuring traffic, serving advertising — and they are not inherently good or bad. What matters is transparency: knowing who sets them, why, and how you can control them.

BuildMetric uses four categories of cookies. The first two are set by us directly and are essential to the site working. The last two are set by third-party services (analytics and advertising) and are subject to your consent, which we collect through the cookie banner shown on your first visit.

Category Examples Duration Consent
Strictly necessary Cookie consent preferences 12 months Always on
Functional Preferred unit system (imperial / metric), theme Session Always on
Analytics Anonymised page views, session data (see Section 04) Up to 24 months Optional
Advertising Set by ad networks (see Section 05) Up to 13 months Optional

You control the optional categories. On your first visit, a banner asks you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. If you reject, only the strictly necessary and functional cookies are set, and analytics and advertising scripts are blocked entirely. You can change your preference at any time from the cookie settings link in the footer, and you can also clear cookies through your browser at any time.

In the EU, UK and California, the banner is configured to be compliant with GDPR, UK GDPR and CPRA respectively — meaning no optional cookie is set before you actively opt in. For more detail on the specific cookies we use and their individual lifespans, see our Cookie Policy.

Your Choice

Rejecting analytics and advertising cookies has no effect on the calculators or content. All tools work identically whether you accept them or not.

Third-Party Services & Analytics

No modern website runs without third-party services. The fonts, the performance layer, the analytics, the advertising — each is delivered by a company other than the one you are reading. We think you deserve to know exactly which providers we use, what they process and what safeguards are in place. The table below covers all the third-party services that touch buildmetric.org in a way that involves your data.

Service Purpose Data Processed Privacy
Google Analytics Traffic analytics Anonymised IP, page views, referrer, browser google.com/privacy
Google AdSense Display advertising Cookies, ad identifiers, coarse geolocation google.com/ads
Google Fonts Typography delivery IP, browser (font file request) google.com/privacy
Cloudflare CDN, DDoS protection IP, request headers, security challenge cookies cloudflare.com
Affiliate networks Purchase tracking (see Section 05) Click ID, referrer, product SKU Varies — disclosed at link

Each provider is bound by its own privacy policy and, where applicable, by a data processing agreement (DPA) with us. For Google services specifically, we have enabled IP anonymisation in Google Analytics so that the last octet of your IP address is discarded before storage. We do not use Google Analytics 4's user-ID feature, cross-device tracking or Google Signals — features that would allow Google to combine data across sites.

For Cloudflare, the security layer that protects the site from DDoS attacks and abuse, some request data is processed at the edge before it reaches our servers. Cloudflare's security cookies are strictly necessary — they prevent attacks — and expire quickly. They do not track you across sites.

You can block these services entirely with browser extensions such as uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger. Doing so will not break the calculators — the only effect is that analytics will not receive your visit and some ads will not appear. We do not attempt to detect or bypass ad blockers.

Good to Know

Blocking analytics and advertising scripts has no effect on calculator functionality. Every tool works the same whether these services are loaded or not.

Section 05

Advertising & Affiliates

BuildMetric is free to use, and it is funded primarily through advertising and, on some pages, affiliate commissions. This is not a hidden fact — it is disclosed at the top of every page that contains affiliate links, and our ads are always visibly marked as "Ad Space" or "Advertisement." What we will not do is let advertising compromise the accuracy or honesty of our editorial content. That is a structural commitment, not a marketing line.

5.1 Display advertising

Display ads on BuildMetric are served through Google AdSense and, from time to time, other reputable ad networks. These networks use cookies and similar technologies to select and measure ads, and they may use the data they collect — including your approximate location, device type and browsing behaviour — to serve ads that are more likely to be relevant to you. We do not control this data processing directly, but we choose our ad partners carefully and configure their settings to minimise tracking.

We have specifically configured our AdSense settings to:

  • Disable personalised advertising for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland unless they have given consent through the Google-certified consent flow.
  • Exclude ads from sensitive categories (gambling, alcohol, political advertising, weight loss) in line with Google's publisher policies.
  • Block any ad that attempts to overlay calculator results, auto-play audio, or use interstitial formats on mobile.
  • Reject ads for products or services we consider misleading or unsafe for a construction industry audience.

5.2 Affiliate links

Some of our guides reference specific products — a particular membrane, a tool, a safety accessory. When we link to a retailer for one of these products, the link may be an affiliate link. If you buy through that link, we receive a small commission from the retailer at no extra cost to you. The commission rate varies by product and retailer.

We want to be very clear about what this does not mean:

  • We do not recommend products because they have an affiliate program. We recommend products we would recommend anyway, based on field experience or published specifications.
  • We do not reorder recommendations to favour higher-commission products. If two products are equally suitable, we may mention both.
  • We do not accept paid placements. A retailer cannot pay to have their product featured in a guide.
  • Affiliate relationships do not influence calculator formulas, editorial conclusions, code references or rankings — ever.

Every page that contains affiliate links displays a disclosure notice at the top, before the first link. If you prefer not to use affiliate links, you can generally find the same product by searching the manufacturer's name directly.

Affiliate Disclosure

Affiliate links are always disclosed before the first link on any page. Advertising partners and affiliates have no editorial input, and no ability to influence calculator results, formula selection or written conclusions.

Section 06

Data Retention & Security

Data we retain is retained only as long as necessary. Data we protect is protected to a standard appropriate to its sensitivity. Both of these principles sound obvious when written down — but they are frequently violated, and we want to be concrete about how they apply in practice.

6.1 How long we keep data

Data Type Retention Period Reason
Server logs (IP, UA) Up to 30 days Security, abuse detection
Analytics events Up to 24 months Traffic trends, editorial planning
Contact form submissions Up to 24 months Support history, correction log
Correction records Indefinite (anonymised) Public version history
Cookie consent preferences 12 months Remembering your choice

When a retention period expires, data is deleted or anonymised automatically. For correction records, the specific bug or error is retained indefinitely in our version history — but the reporter's personal information is redacted or removed after the standard 24-month period, and is only kept longer with explicit consent.

6.2 How we protect data

Data in transit between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3 (HTTPS), and we enforce HSTS to prevent downgrade attacks. Where data is stored on our servers, access is restricted to a small number of personnel and protected by multi-factor authentication. We do not store payment information, because we have no payment system.

The most significant protection measure is architectural: we collect very little in the first place. Calculators run in your browser — your inputs never reach us. Contact form data is stored briefly and then deleted. There is no login system that could be compromised. There is no customer database of personal information to be leaked. The most reliable way to prevent a data breach is not to hold data — and we take that principle seriously.

In the unlikely event of a breach that affects personal data, we will notify affected users and, where required by law, the relevant supervisory authority (the ICO in the UK, the relevant state AG in California, the OAIC in Australia, and the relevant EU DPA) within the timeframes required by the applicable regulation.

Design Principle

The safest data is data we never collect. We collect only what we need to operate the site and respond to your messages — nothing more.

Section 08

International Transfers & Children

8.1 International data transfers

BuildMetric is operated from and served to users in multiple countries. Because the internet is global by nature, your data may be processed in jurisdictions other than the one in which you live. When this happens, we apply the safeguards described below.

Our primary hosting and security layer is Cloudflare, which operates a global edge network. Cloudflare processes request data at the edge node closest to you to ensure fast, secure delivery — this may mean your IP and request headers are briefly processed in a country other than your own. Cloudflare's data processing is governed by its own DPA, which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers originating in the EEA.

Our analytics (Google Analytics) is operated by Google LLC, which may process data in the United States. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF) and UK Extension, which provides a lawful mechanism for EEA-to-US and UK-to-US data transfers. IP anonymisation is enabled in all our Google Analytics configurations.

Where required by law, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, or the equivalent UK IDTA, as the transfer mechanism for personal data leaving the EEA or UK. For Australian users, we take reasonable steps consistent with Australian Privacy Principle 8 to ensure overseas recipients handle your data in a manner consistent with the APPs.

8.2 Children's privacy

BuildMetric is a professional tool intended for adults working in or studying the construction industry. The site is not directed at children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16 — or under 13 in the United States, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Because we do not have an account system, a login process, or any interactive feature that requires personal data to use, the site is structurally minimal in terms of children's data risk. We do not knowingly process personal information from minors, and we do not use age-gating mechanisms that would inadvertently encourage children to falsify their age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information — for example, via the contact form — please email contact@buildmetric.org with the subject line "Child Data Request". We will delete the relevant records within five business days of verifying the request.

Age Notice

BuildMetric is intended for adults. If you are under 16 (or under 13 in the US), please do not submit personal information through our contact form or by email.
