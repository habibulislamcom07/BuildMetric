Editorial Standards

Why You Can Trust These Guides

Construction is a high-stakes industry. Bad information costs money and compromises safety. We hold our guides to the same standard as our calculators.

Human-Written

No AI-generated fluff. Every article is written by a member of our editorial team and reviewed by a practising professional before publication.

Code-Aware

We don't just guess. Where relevant, our guides cite the current published standards (IRC 2021, BS 5534, AS 1684) so you can verify the math.

Dated & Versioned

Building codes change. Every guide displays a clear "Last Updated" date so you know exactly when the information was last verified against current codes.

Open to Corrections

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Guides

01 Are these guides written by AI?

No. Every article in our library is written by a member of the BuildMetric editorial team and reviewed by a practising professional (quantity surveyor, structural engineer, or licensed contractor). We do not publish AI-generated content without a named human reviewer, and we do not use AI to produce the core technical explanations.

02 How often are the guides updated?

Guides are reviewed periodically against current code editions, and the "Last Updated" date is displayed on every article. When a standard changes (for example, a new IRC or BS edition), we update the affected guides and note the change in the page's version history.

03 Can I republish or use these guides on my own website?

Short quotes with clear attribution and a working link back to the original page are welcome. You may not, however, reproduce substantial portions of our content, republish the full text of a guide on another site, or use our content for commercial purposes without prior written permission. For licensing requests, email contact@buildmetric.org.

04 Do your guides reference local building codes?

Yes. Because we write for three distinct markets, our guides reference the appropriate regional standards: IRC 2021 and IBC for the United States, BS 5534 and Building Regulations for the UK, and the NCC and AS 1684 for Australia. Where codes differ between these markets, we say so explicitly.

05 Can I suggest a topic for a new guide?

Absolutely. We add new guides every month based on what our readers are building. If you are stuck on a specific calculation or code question, email contact@buildmetric.org with the subject "Guide Suggestion". A working scenario from your site is the fastest way to get it prioritised.

06 Are the guides free to read?

Yes. Every guide and article on BuildMetric is completely free to read. There is no paywall, no forced sign-up, and no gated content. The site is funded through transparent display advertising.

07 Who exactly reviews the technical guides?

Our reviewer group consists of quantity surveyors, structural engineers, and licensed roofing and framing contractors with field experience in the US, UK, and Australian construction industries. Depending on the topic, the article is routed to the relevant specialist.
